Payload 12-Month Marketing Plan

From a strong real estate foundation to a repeatable growth engine

A phased plan to turn Payload's customers, data, and reach into predictable pipeline in real estate, and then expands into adjacent industries with similar needs.

AToday

A leader in earnest money payments with 3,000+ real estate customers, 15+ software integrations, and banking partnerships with J.P. Morgan and Fifth Third.

BIn 12 months

A marketing engine that produces steady pipeline, grows revenue from existing customers, and has a proven path into more industries.

The ambition

Payload becomes the name people think of when they think about moving money in industries that still run on checks and wires.

Real estate is the foundation. Prove out the playbook, scale the motions that work, and then expand into other industries.

How this plan grows Payload

Five levers work together across 4 phases to facilitate growth.

  1. 1

    Grow current customers

    Most customers start with earnest money. Moving them into commissions, rent, agent fees, and vendor payments is a smooth transition into new revenue.

    • Lifecycle Marketing
    • Product Marketing
  2. 2

    Make Payload's data the story

    Payload sees payment and fraud patterns across more than 3,000 real estate customers. A regular Payments Index turns that into news, posts, and sales talking points.

    • PR
    • Thought Leadership and Content
    • Website and Measurement
  3. 3

    Turn interest into sales conversations

    Listening tools find people talking about wire fraud, slow payments, and other pain points. Leaders reach out personally, target accounts get extra attention, and good fits go to sales.

    • Social Listening and Outreach
    • Account-Based Marketing
    • Paid Media
  4. 4

    Build trust through others

    Real estate professionals trust their associations, their software, and their peers. Partners, a free CE course, speaking, and customer stories put Payload in front of them.

    • Partners, Associations, and Events
    • Customer Stories and Advocacy
  5. 5

    Scale the playbook into more industries

    Payload is already winning customers outside real estate. Once the real estate playbook runs smoothly, it rolls into the industries showing traction or where we see opportunity. A formal go or no-go review decides where to put more budget.

    • New Industries

Team and capacity

Built for a lean team of two marketers, AI tools, and part-time specialists.

  • Marketer 1

    Strategy, CEO thought leadership, account-based marketing, paid media, the listening and outreach engine, and reporting.

  • Marketer 2

    Content engine, product marketing, lifecycle marketing, and customer stories.

  • AI tools

    First drafts, repurposing, research, lead scoring, and personalization.

  • Contractors

    Design and video, and paid media setup as needed.

  • Leadership time. About one hour a week.

  • Sales capacity. Lead volume matched to what sales can work.

  • Compliance. Payments content gets reviewed before it goes out.

Capacity rule. No more than 3 or 4 new programs launching at once.

How the engine works

  1. Ideas. Payload's data, its customers, and its leaders create the ideas.
  2. Content. AI turns each idea into dozens of assets.
  3. Distribution. Leaders, customers, partners, press, and paid spread them.
  4. Signals. Listening and tracking show who engages.
  5. Pipeline. Account-based marketing, outreach, and sales start conversations.
  6. Growth. Lifecycle marketing grows each customer, and their stories restart the cycle.

Measurement

Pipeline and revenue

  • Pipeline and revenue by industry, channel, and program
  • Cost per opportunity by channel
  • Meetings from outreach and target accounts

Customer growth

  • Time to first payment for new customers
  • Payment types and volume per customer
  • Adoption among software partners' users

Reach and reputation

  • Reach and engagement for C-suite and other leaders
  • Trade press coverage
  • Presence in AI search answers

  1. Whether product usage data reaches the CRM

  5. What review public content about payments needs

  7. What gets engagement on leadership posts, and who is engaging
