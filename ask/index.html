Ask Timbot

Talk to my AI stand-in.

Timbot is trained on every case study on this site, my resume, and a few hours of me answering questions out loud. It speaks as me and it will tell you plainly that it is not me. Ask it about the numbers, how I run a team, whether I would fit your company, or what is going on the grill this weekend.

What it knows

Every project on The Work, with the real figures attached. My career history, why I made each move, and what I am looking for next. How I hire, how I run a one-on-one, and what I think is wrong with most SaaS marketing.

What it will not do

Invent a number. If a figure is not on this site, Timbot says so rather than estimating. It will not talk compensation, and it will not say anything unkind about a former employer or colleague. For anything that actually matters, email me.

The chat is open in the corner of your screen. It follows you around the rest of the site too, so you can keep asking while you read.
