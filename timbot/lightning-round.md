# Lightning round

Thirty questions, one breath each. Should take six or seven minutes total.

**Rules:** answer fast, don't explain, don't hedge. If a question makes you want
to add a sentence, add it. Those are the ones that end up being the good
answers, and the reason to record this rather than type it.

Say "pass" and keep moving on anything you don't want on file.

---

## Round 1: personality

1. Guilty pleasure TV show.
2. Favorite snack.
3. Favorite candy.
4. Coffee order.
5. Comfort movie, the one you'll rewatch anytime.
6. Walk-up song if you had one.
7. Best concert you've been to.
8. Pizza topping, and are you willing to defend it.
9. What's going on the grill this weekend.
10. Best trip you've taken.
11. Favorite holiday.
12. Last thing you googled.
13. Most-used emoji.
14. A word you overuse.
15. Superpower you'd actually pick.

## Round 2: professional

16. Favorite marketing channel.
17. Most overrated marketing metric.
18. Best SaaS website right now.
19. Worst marketing buzzword.
20. The first thing you check every morning.
21. Most underrated marketing skill.
22. Biggest waste of marketing budget you see over and over.
23. One marketing practice you'd delete forever.
24. Best subject line you've ever written.
25. Day one at a new company. What's your first move.
26. An AI prompt you reuse constantly.

## Round 3: this or that

Just pick. No explaining unless you can't help it.

27. Grill or smoker. Mountains or beach. Book or audiobook. Fiction or
    nonfiction.
28. SEO or paid. Brand or demand. Fix the funnel or feed the funnel. Generalist
    or specialist.
29. Big launch or steady drip. Spreadsheet or doc. Slack or email. Dark mode or
    light mode.
30. Early meeting or late meeting. Inbox zero or inbox chaos. Notebook or app.
    Morning person or night person.

---

Same as before, raw transcript, no cleanup. These go into `persona.md` rather
than `facts.md`, since they're voice and texture rather than claims anyone needs
to verify.
